# Semplicita Modern attribution

The storefront redistributes the original, unmodified Semplicita Modern Book and Book Italic OpenType files.

- Original typeface: Semplicita, designed by Alessandro Butti for the Nebiolo foundry.
- Digital reconstruction and porting: Leonardo Di Lena (Flanker / Studio Di Lena).
- Included files: `app/static/fonts/semplicita-book.otf` and `app/static/fonts/semplicita-book-italic.otf`.
- Source: <https://www.studiodilena.com/en/semplicita-font.html>

The font files are included unchanged. The full license text is in `CC-BY-ND-3.0.txt`.
